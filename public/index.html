ILLEGALSOCCER

3v3 street soccer. No referee. No rules. Slide tackles strongly encouraged.

You play Blaze. Your two teammates and the whole rival team are AI.

or play with friends

How it works

  • Up to 6 humans. 3 per team. Any empty seat is played by the AI, so you can go solo, team up against the AI, or go head-to-head.
  • Everyone moves at once. Each turn every player secretly picks a move, then it all happens together.
  • The odds are real. Distance, stats, stamina and what your opponents chose decide every roll.
  • No fouls. Slide tackle the carrier to steal it, or flatten anyone to cancel their move.
  • Watch it in 3D. Every turn replays on a floodlit street pitch: tackles, passes, shots, keeper dives. Pick your camera, or switch to 2D.

      How to play

      Each match is a set number of turns. Every turn, all six players pick a move at the same time. The turn then plays out in order:

      1. Mind Read, then the other specials (fastest player first)
      2. Slide tackles (fastest first). Get flattened and your move is cancelled.
      3. Runs and marking
      4. The ball carrier's move: dribble, pass, shoot, power shot or special shot
      5. Everyone else drifts into position. Stamina and cooldowns tick.

      Moves

      Dribble
      Carry the ball 2 to 3 cells. Defenders nearby (and marking you) make it harder.
      Pass
      Long passes, marked receivers and defenders in the passing lane all cut the odds. Passing to a teammate who just made a run gets a bonus.
      Shoot
      Accurate up close, falls off quickly with distance and pressure.
      Power Shot
      Loses far less to distance and pressure. Costs 22 stamina, 2-turn cooldown.
      Slide Tackle
      Hit the carrier to steal the ball, or anyone else to flatten them and cancel their move. Miss and you're on the floor next turn.
      Mark
      Defenders only. Shadow someone: passes to them, their shots and their dribbles all get harder.
      Make Run
      Attackers only. Sprint into space and become open for a pass this turn.
      Recover
      +20 extra stamina, but you don't put any pressure on anyone.
      Special
      Every player has one. They cost a lot of stamina and have long cooldowns.

      Below 25 stamina you're gassed: everything is 10% worse. Getting knocked down leaves you dazed (−12%) for a turn. The odds on the buttons are estimates; the real roll also includes what your opponents secretly chose.
